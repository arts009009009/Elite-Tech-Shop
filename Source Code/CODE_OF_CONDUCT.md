# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (Elite Tech Shop Edition)

## Our Pledge
We, as members, contributors, and leaders, pledge to make participation in this community a harassment‑free, welcoming experience for everyone.  
No matter your background, identity, or level of experience, you’re part of the chaos here — and we commit to keeping it safe, inclusive, and respectful.

## Our Standards
Positive behaviors include:
- Showing empathy and kindness toward others  
- Respecting different opinions and viewpoints  
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully  
- Owning mistakes, apologizing, and learning from them  
- Focusing on what benefits the community as a whole  

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Sexualized language, imagery, or advances  
- Trolling, insults, or personal/political attacks  
- Harassment (public or private)  
- Publishing private information without consent  
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or collaborative setting  

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope
This Code applies to all community spaces and when representing the project in public (e.g., official accounts, events, or communications).

## Enforcement
Report violations to: **arts009009009@outlook.com**.  
All complaints will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for privacy.

## Enforcement Guidelines
1. **Correction**  
   *Impact*: Minor inappropriate language or behavior.  
   *Consequence*: Private written warning; public apology may be requested.  

2. **Warning**  
   *Impact*: Single incident or repeated minor violations.  
   *Consequence*: Formal warning; restricted interaction for a set period.  

3. **Temporary Ban**  
   *Impact*: Serious violation or sustained inappropriate behavior.  
   *Consequence*: Temporary ban from community interaction.  

4. **Permanent Ban**  
   *Impact*: Pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression.  
   *Consequence*: Permanent removal from community spaces.
